🔬 Deep-Dive: The Forensic Methodology

Our detection framework combines spatial feature representations with frequency-domain residual analysis to catch generative artifacts left behind by Diffusion models, GANs, and face-swapping engines.

1. Preprocessing & Normalization

Uploaded images are securely converted to RGB, resized to 224×224 pixels, and normalized using standard ImageNet parameters ([0.485, 0.456, 0.406]).

2. EfficientNet-B0 Inference

Mobile Inverted Bottleneck convolutions (MBConv) with Squeeze-and-Excitation attention extract multi-scale spatial features with high parameter efficiency.

3. Calibrated Thresholding

Softmax probabilities are evaluated against an optimized decision threshold determined during validation to maximize balanced F1-score accuracy.
