About DeepfakeDetection

DeepfakeDetection is a cutting-edge, artificial intelligence-driven platform dedicated to verifying visual authenticity. In an era where generative media is becoming indistinguishable from actual photography, our mission is to empower individuals, media outlets, and organizations with the tools to defend truth.

By combining advanced Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) with pixel integrity analysis, our platform highlights subtle structural manipulation, color profile anomalies, and synthesis artifacts that are invisible to the human eye.
